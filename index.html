The context

A business with potential, and a process that needed structure

While working in business development at Socheko.com, I found problems in four areas: vendor management, sales processes, payment arrangements and product organisation.

Vendor agreements

Existing vendor agreements were inconsistent in structure and management.

Read more

Agreements with existing vendors varied in margins, terms and conditions, and delivery arrangements. Contracts weren’t reviewed regularly, which made vendor relationships and expectations harder to manage.

Payment and commercial terms

Margins, payment terms and commercial procedures needed clearer definition.

Read more

Margin ratios, payment terms and operational procedures needed to be documented and standardised. Without consistent commercial guidelines, misunderstandings and disagreements with vendors were increasing.

Product categorisation

Vendors and their products needed a more systematic category structure.

Read more

We needed a consistent category structure to organise products and assign vendors by what they supplied, and a dedicated category manager was appointed for this work. Vendors also had to provide accurate product information, descriptions and high-quality photos in the website’s required format, so listings stayed consistent.

Customer needs

Customer feedback needed to feed into product and business decisions.

Read more

Customer Service conversations revealed preferences, recurring issues and unmet needs. That information had to reach the right teams to guide product selection, priorities and business improvements.

      The outcomes

      What changed

      Better vendor coordination, clearer commercial processes, organised products and real use of customer feedback gave us a more structured way to manage vendors and make business decisions.

      Vendor relationships, agreements and product categories became systematically organised, so responsibilities and information were easier to manage.

      Fewer payment issues

      Clearer payment terms, margin ratios and agreements reduced misunderstandings and payment problems with vendors.

      Better-organised vendors, more complete product information and attention to customer needs supported product selection, sales and customer satisfaction.
